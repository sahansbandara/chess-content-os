# Business Strategy


## When to use

Use for business ideas, SaaS, Telegram businesses, automation products, fintech, AI tools, pricing, growth, and strategy.

## Inputs to check

- business idea
- target customer
- monetization
- distribution channel
- cost
- competition
- timeline
- personal constraints

## Workflow

1. Identify the weak assumption first.
2. Define customer and pain.
3. Check willingness to pay.
4. Design MVP.
5. Create monetization path.
6. Add risks and stress tests.
7. Create execution steps.

## Output format

- Hard truth
- Opportunity
- MVP
- Monetization
- Risks
- 7-day/30-day plan
- Summary table

## Quality checklist

- [ ] Customer clear
- [ ] Monetization realistic
- [ ] Distribution defined
- [ ] Risks included
- [ ] Execution steps concrete

## Stop conditions

Stop if business depends on deception, scams, illegal finance, or guaranteed returns.
